# Assets

Upload these to your Discord application under **Rich Presence → Art Assets**.
The asset *key* you give them in Discord is what goes into `config.yaml`.

| File | Discord asset key | Used as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kimi_logo.png` | `kimi_logo` | `discord.large_image` |
| optional badge | your choice | `discord.small_image` |

Requirements: PNG, at least 512×512 (`kimi_logo.png` here is 1280×1280). Discord can take a few minutes
to serve a newly uploaded asset.
